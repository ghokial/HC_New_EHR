# Privacy and data safety

The app handles student and family identity, contact, attendance, health, medication, documents, and communications as sensitive data.

- Enforce family/facility access on the server; interface filtering is not authorization.
- Use HTTPS and encryption at rest.
- Keep credentials/tokens out of source, logs, screenshots, crash reports, and backups.
- Do not add tracking, ads, or analytics unless approved and disclosed.
- Provide privacy, support, access/correction, retention, and deletion workflows.
- Verify FERPA, COPPA, and applicable health/privacy requirements with qualified counsel.
- Camera/photo access is optional and only for family-selected uploads.
- Complete Apple App Privacy and Google Play Data safety from actual production behavior.
